# SR Electronics Park — Unified Treasury, POS & ERP Hub

An enterprise-grade Point of Sale (POS), treasury, and inventory management system built for multi-brand electronics retail operations (including MyOne, Minister, and Butterfly Group dealerships).

## Core Capabilities
- **Unified POS & Billing:** Fast retail checkout, installment tracking, and customer ledger management.
- **Inventory & SKU Velocity:** Real-time stock monitoring across multiple product lines to minimize dead stock and optimize working capital.
- **Treasury & Financial Reconciliation:** Automated daily cash-flow tracking, supplier payables, and margin analytics.

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend & Logic:** JavaScript, HTML5, CSS3
- **Architecture:** Modular ERP & Treasury Dashboard

## Strategic Context
Developed under the **Salsabilah Amin Empires** ecosystem to bridge applied data analytics with high-volume consumer electronics distribution.
